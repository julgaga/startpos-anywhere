# v1.0.0

- First release.
- Automatic recording of your runs, one snapshot per block.
- Respawn, % slider and back-to-start buttons in the pause menu.
- Correct floor, ceiling and camera when spawning in ship, ball, UFO, wave, spider and swing.
- Manual start positions with configurable keys, a minimum gap and a "delete all" button.
- Per-level saving, discarded when the level is updated.
